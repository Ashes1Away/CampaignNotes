## Background
Minor forces from the [[Black Nebula]] had long held the pirate stronghold of [[Echo Station]]. Offering it as a safe haven to any and all pirate forces, no matter their faction. 

What had been home to just a handful of Black Nebula had rapidly grown in recent times with the rumoured reappearance of [[Captain Varn Blackclaw|Blackclaw]]. The number of members had grown rapidly and reports were leaving of newly erected statues to Blackclaw to mark his heroic return.

Seeing this resurgence in numbers [[Admiral Lysander Trask]] believed left unchecked the pirate stronghold would soon swell to unimaginable numbers, and so in an attempt to quell the problem early and send a message to all pirate factions he launched a siege.
## Situation
[[Admiral Lysander Trask|Admiral Trask]] assembled the forces of the [[Starlance Coalition]] just outside the [[Nebula Bubble]] of [[Echo Station]]. He took pride of place in the vanguard atop his personal dreadnought [[The Aegis]]. Reports state that he was flanked by 25 ships abreast. The full might of the coalition had been summoned to siege.

Before they had the chance to be spotted by scouts, the coalition advanced upon the stronghold. The sudden and unexpected attack had the desired outcome as panic ensued within [[Echo Station]]. Various pirate ships began to flee only to be consumed by the onslaught from the coalition's cannons.

Just as [[Admiral Lysander Trask|Trask]] was about to start celebrating his success, fire returned from the stronghold itself. Built around a large ancient weapons system and with the immediate panic having died down the [[Black Nebula]] were ready to defend their home. 

The ensuing battle and cannon fire was visible across the astral sea, as the coalition lost ship after ship. Eventually silence fell the overwhelming might of the Aegis had silenced the archaic weapon and a full scale invasion had begun. The stronghold was largely empty with most pirates having fled or perished in the battle. Much to the chagrin of [[Admiral Lysander Trask|Trask]] the [[Black Nebula]] leadership in charge of the weapon had emergency warped away. 
## Outcome
Despite the large losses and lack of any prisoners, the siege had been a great success as it showed the entire Astral that the pirates were not safe anywhere anymore. The station remains in possession of the [[Starlance Coalition|Coalition]] and has become [[Admiral Lysander Trask|Admiral Trask's]] personal seat of power in these troubled times with many reports surfacing he is trying to rebuild the mechanism that ripped apart his fleet many years ago.
